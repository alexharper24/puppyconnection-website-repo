---
breed: basset-hound
intro: The Basset Hound is a short-legged Hound Group breed, and its standard calls it the heaviest-boned dog for its size.
---
Low and heavy-boned. | Grown Bassets weigh 40 to 65 pounds, and the standard asks for 14 inches or less at the shoulder.
Weekly brushing. | The short coat can shed heavily, so AKC advises a soft brush or shedding tool at least once a week.
Good with kids. | AKC scores Bassets 5 of 5 with young children, and the standard calls them mild, never sharp or timid.
Health checks. | Bassets live 12 to 13 years, and the club recommends an eye exam and DNA tests, glaucoma among them.
