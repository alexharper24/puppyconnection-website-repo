---
breed: papillon
intro: The Papillon is a small, fine-boned toy dog named for its large, wing-shaped ears, since papillon is French for butterfly.
---
Tiny. | Grown Papillons stand 8 to 11 inches and weigh 5 to 10 pounds.
Easy coat. | The silky coat has no undercoat, so AKC says grooming every month or so is enough.
Lively and vocal. | AKC scores the breed 5 of 5 for trainability and barking, and 4 for energy.
Long life. | Papillons usually live 14 to 16 years, and AKC warns a small child can hurt a puppy by accident.
