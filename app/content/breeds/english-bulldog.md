---
breed: english-bulldog
intro: The English Bulldog, registered with AKC as the Bulldog, is a low, heavy, short-faced dog that AKC scores low for barking.
---
Heavy build. | Bulldogs stand 14 to 15 inches, and AKC lists about 50 pounds for a male and 40 for a female.
Moderate shedding. | The short, smooth coat scores 3 of 5 on AKC's shedding scale.
Heat sensitive. | A Bulldog must not overheat, so keep shade and water handy and never leave it in a closed car.
Shorter life. | Expect 8 to 10 years, and ask whether the parents had the club's knee, heart and windpipe checks.
