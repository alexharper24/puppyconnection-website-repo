Puppy Connection email designs

Three design directions for the emails the site sends, each shown for a password reset, a breeder invitation and a mailing-list email, at desktop width and at phone width (375px). Pick one direction, or name the parts you like from each.

A. Clean and quietB. Warm brand bandC. Personal noteContrast checks

The password and invitation wording is the current wording from the breeder portal, unchanged. The mailing-list email body is sample text announcing the new site. The business name "Sample Kennel" and every link token are samples too. In the real emails the logo loads from the live site by its full web address, and each email also goes out with its plain-text version as it does today.

Direction A

Clean and quiet

A white card on a warm page with the dark logo at the top and one olive button, so the link is the only thing that stands out. It looks the same in every inbox and reads well with images off, because almost nothing depends on the logo.

Direction B

Warm brand band

A charcoal band carries the white logo and a gold line, the same look as the site header, and the gold button matches the site's buttons. It is the most recognizable of the three, and the band and button keep their colors when images are off.

Direction C

Personal note

A small logo, a short warm opening line and a signature from Amber and the team make each email read like a note from a person, which suits the mailing list and invitations best. The opening lines shown here are sample wording for Amber to rewrite in her own voice, and the automatic-message line stays in the footer of the portal emails.

Color contrast checks

Every text and background pair used in the three designs, measured against WCAG 2.1 AA. Normal text needs 4.5 to 1, and large text (24px, or 18.7px bold) needs 3 to 1. The ratios below are calculated by this page from the hex values, so they stay true if a color changes. The bronze rule in Direction C is decoration and carries no text.
