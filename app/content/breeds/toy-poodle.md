---
breed: toy-poodle
intro: The Toy Poodle is the smallest of the three Poodle sizes, 10 inches or under at the shoulder with a dense, curly coat.
---
Tiny. | Grown Toy Poodles weigh 4 to 6 pounds, and one over 10 inches counts as a Miniature.
Barely sheds. | Shedding scores 1 of 5, the lowest, while grooming scores 4 because the coat keeps growing.
Easy to train. | AKC rates Toy Poodles 5 of 5 for trainability, but toddlers need supervising with a dog this small.
Long life. | Toy Poodles live 10 to 18 years, and AKC says loose kneecaps are more likely than in Standard Poodles.
