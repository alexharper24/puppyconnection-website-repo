Yorkshire Terrier Breed Information
The Yorkshire Terrier: A Tiny Dog with a Big Attitude
Don’t let the petite size fool you—Yorkshire Terriers (or “Yorkies”) pack a ton of personality into their small frames. Known for their silky coats, sparkly eyes, and spirited nature, Yorkies are one of the most popular toy breeds in the world. They’re bold, affectionate, and always ready to strut their stuff. Whether nestled in a designer bag or confidently leading the way on a walk, the Yorkie lives life with flair.
A Glamorous Look with Classic Charm
Yorkies typically weigh around 4 to 7 pounds and stand about 7 to 8 inches tall, but their presence feels much bigger. Their long, flowing coats are a signature feature—often steel blue and tan in color, soft as silk, and full of elegance. Many Yorkie owners choose to keep the coat trimmed short for easy maintenance, though those growing it out for show know how much pride (and brushing!) comes with that stunning look.
Despite their dainty appearance, Yorkies are sturdier than they seem, full of zest and energy.
Bold, Bright, and Full of Sass
Yorkshire Terriers are fearless and feisty, with a confidence that rivals breeds ten times their size. They are smart, curious, and often act as if they rule the roost. But beneath that bold exterior is a deeply affectionate little companion who adores their people.
Yorkies love being close—preferably on your lap, tucked under your arm, or following you from room to room. They can be a bit vocal, alerting you to every passerby or unexpected sound, but their intent is always to protect their beloved humans.
Perfect for City Life and Cozy Homes
Thanks to their compact size, Yorkies are perfect for apartment dwellers, urban living, or anyone with limited space. They don’t need much room to be happy, but they do need attention, love, and daily activity to burn off their energy. A brisk walk, indoor playtime, or a puzzle toy can keep your Yorkie both mentally and physically engaged.
They’re typically good with older children who know how to handle small dogs gently and can get along with other pets, especially when socialized early.
Trainable—With a Dash of Independence
Yorkies are intelligent and quick to learn, but they often have a mind of their own. Early socialization and positive, consistent training help shape a polite, confident pup. Because of their terrier roots, Yorkies can be a bit stubborn and strong-willed, but with patience and a little humor, they become delightful and obedient companions.
Potty training can sometimes take a little longer, so crate training and a reliable routine are your best friends during puppyhood.
A Devoted and Dazzling Companion
If you’re looking for a dog that’s both glamorous and gutsy, the Yorkshire Terrier is a fantastic fit. These pint-sized pups are loyal, loving, and endlessly entertaining.
From their elegant appearance to their lively spirit, Yorkies are dogs that shine bright in any home. Whether they’re perched on your lap, bouncing down the hallway, or giving you a cheeky bark to announce dinner time, they’re sure to keep life interesting—and full of love.

Top 5 Frequently Asked Questions About Yorkshire Terriers

Are Yorkies good family dogs?
Yes, Yorkshire Terriers (or “Yorkies”) can make fantastic family companions, especially in homes that appreciate their spunky, affectionate nature. They’re deeply loyal to their people and love being part of the action. Yorkies often form strong bonds with one or two individuals, but they can do well with respectful children and other pets when properly socialized. Because of their small size, they’re best suited to families that can supervise play and keep things gentle—especially around young kids.
Do Yorkies require a lot of grooming?
Yes, Yorkies have a long, silky coat that requires regular grooming, especially if kept in its full length. Their hair is more like human hair than fur, so it tangles easily and needs daily brushing to prevent mats. Many owners opt for a shorter “puppy cut” to make maintenance easier. In either case, they’ll need routine bathing, trimming, and regular care for their ears, nails, and teeth. Keeping their facial hair clean and trimmed is also important, especially around the eyes and mouth.
Are Yorkies easy to train?
Yorkies are very smart and eager to learn, but they can also be a bit feisty and independent. They respond well to positive reinforcement—like treats, praise, and play—but consistency is key, especially when it comes to housebreaking. Their small size can make potty training a challenge, so patience is a must. They’re great at learning tricks and commands, and many even enjoy agility or obedience training. Just keep sessions short, upbeat, and fun!
Do Yorkies have health issues?
Like many toy breeds, Yorkies can be prone to a few specific health conditions. These may include dental issues, luxating patellas (loose kneecaps), hypoglycemia (especially in puppies), tracheal collapse, and liver shunt. Their small mouths often lead to crowded teeth, so regular dental care is essential. Choosing a puppy from a reputable breeder who screens for genetic issues is an important step toward long-term health. Routine vet care and a high-quality diet also help keep them thriving.
How much exercise do Yorkies need?
While they’re small, Yorkies are surprisingly energetic and need daily exercise to stay happy and well-behaved. A couple of short walks, some indoor playtime, and a few training games usually do the trick. They love to explore and often act like little watchdogs, always alert and curious. Because they’re so portable, Yorkies make great travel or shopping companions and enjoy being involved in whatever you're doing. Mental stimulation is just as important as physical activity for this clever little breed.
