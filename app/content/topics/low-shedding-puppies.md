Although no dog is truly hypoallergenic, because the proteins people react to come from a dog's saliva and dander and are carried on its hair, some breeds shed far less than others. Less shedding means less hair carrying those proteins around the house, and this guide walks through the breeds that shed least, with puppies from those breeds listed on Puppy Connection right now.

## Are any dogs hypoallergenic?

The American College of Allergy, Asthma and Immunology says all dogs produce allergens and no breed has been shown to be hypoallergenic. The proteins are made in a dog's saliva, dander and urine, they cling to its hair, and the length of a coat does not change how allergenic a dog is. A 2012 study found that so-called hypoallergenic breeds carried as much allergen in their coats as other dogs, or more, and their homes had no less allergen in the air.

## Which breeds shed the least?

On AKC's five-point shedding scale, where 1 means no shedding, the Toy, Miniature and Standard Poodle, the Shih Tzu, the Maltese and the Yorkshire Terrier all score a 1. The Havanese, the Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, the Pomeranian, the Dachshund and the Boston Terrier score 2.

Low shedding usually means more grooming. Poodles, Shih Tzus and Maltese score 4 for grooming and Yorkies score 5, because hair that stays on the dog needs brushing and clipping.

These small breeds grow long or wiry coats that hold on to their hair, so expect regular brushing and trims.

@shih-tzu: AKC scores it 1 of 5 for shedding and 4 of 5 for grooming.
@maltese: AKC scores it 1 of 5 for shedding and 4 of 5 for grooming.
@yorkshire-terrier: AKC scores it 1 of 5 for shedding and 5 of 5 for grooming, the most frequent.

@miniature-schnauzer: AKC scores it 3 of 5 for shedding, and lists it among its breeds for people with allergies.

{{group: Doodles and other crosses | shih-poo, teddy-bear-shihchon, cavapoo, mini-bernedoodle, munchkin-bernedoodle, f1b-mini-goldendoodle}}

A cross has no breed standard of its own, so its coat depends on what each puppy inherits from its parents. In a genetic study of about 1,500 dogs from 124 breeds, the low-shedding breeds carried the furnishings gene, which gives the beard and eyebrows, and a doodle that misses it grows what breeders call an improper coat. Ask a doodle breeder whether the parents have been tested for furnishings.

@shih-poo: A Shih Tzu and Poodle cross, and AKC scores both parent breeds 1 of 5 for shedding.
@teddy-bear-shihchon: A Shih Tzu and Bichon Frise cross, and AKC scores both parent breeds 1 of 5 for shedding.
@cavapoo: A Cavalier and Poodle cross, so a puppy can take after the low-shedding Poodle or the Cavalier.
@mini-bernedoodle: A Bernese Mountain Dog and Miniature Poodle cross, and the Bernese scores 5 of 5 for shedding, so coats in a litter can range widely.
@munchkin-bernedoodle: A Bernese and Poodle line bred small, so each puppy's coat follows its own parents.
@f1b-mini-goldendoodle: A Goldendoodle bred back to a Poodle or Golden, and the Golden scores 4 of 5 for shedding, so ask about the parents' coats.

## If someone in the house has allergies

Talk to an allergist before you commit, and spend time around the breed, or better the parents, before putting down a deposit. A lower-shedding dog means less hair on the floor, and it cannot promise more than that.
