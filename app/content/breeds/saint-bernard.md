---
breed: saint-bernard
intro: The Saint Bernard is a giant Working Group dog that comes in a smooth coat or a rough one.
---
Giant. | Grown Saints weigh 120 to 180 pounds and stand 26 to 30 inches at the shoulder.
Moderate shedding. | AKC scores the coat 3 of 5 for shedding and 2 for grooming.
Good with kids. | AKC rates Saints 5 of 5 with young children and 1 for barking, the quietest score.
Shorter life. | Saints usually live 8 to 10 years, and AKC warns that deep-chested breeds can develop bloat.
