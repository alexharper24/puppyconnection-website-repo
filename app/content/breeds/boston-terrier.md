---
breed: boston-terrier
intro: Small and short-coated, the Boston Terrier comes from the AKC Non-Sporting Group and is marked in black, brindle or seal with white.
---
Small. | Grown Bostons stand 10 to 12 inches and weigh 12 to 25 pounds.
Easy coat. | The short, smooth coat scores 2 of 5 for shedding and needs only an occasional brush.
Family friendly. | On AKC's scales it earns top marks with young children and just 2 of 5 for barking.
Watch the heat. | AKC notes that flat-faced breeds like the Boston can have trouble breathing in hot weather.
