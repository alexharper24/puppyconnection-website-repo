Mervin
My family has been raising puppies for 5 1/2 years and we are looking forward to many more years. We have 7 children whom  give our puppies lots of quality time and attention.
We remove dewclaws as well as dock tails when it's breed is appropriate.
Our puppies will go for their first vet exam at 7 weeks.
Vaccination Protocol - Our puppies will have their dewormings and vaccations started. We give them Pyrantel, Safeguard, Ivermectin, Neo-par, 5 Way and Intractrac 3
What we send home  -
Vaccination record, AKC papers, and sample of Eukanuba puppy food.
We reserve Sundays for church and family, so we are not available for phone calls or visits on that day.
based in northern Indiana
