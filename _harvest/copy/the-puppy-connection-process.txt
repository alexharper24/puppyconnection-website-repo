How to adopt a puppy from Puppy Connection
At Puppy Connection, we bring responsible breeders and loving families together — one precious paw at a time.
We provide clear communication, safe and easy next steps, and lifetime support.
From your first video call to the day the puppy arrives — here’s exactly how Puppy Connection seeks to support you, safeguard our puppies, and ensure a smooth transition.

Here's an in-depth look:
Q: How long is a typical video call?
A: 10–15 minutes. That’s enough to meet the breeder, see the puppies and parents, and ask questions.
Q: Can I bring my kids/guests to an in-person visit?
A: Yes, with prior approval. We ask that visitors follow breeder guidelines for safety and hygiene.
Q: What if I can’t make the scheduled time?
A: Use the calendar link in your confirmation email or simply send us a text to reschedule up to 2 hours before the appointment
Step 1 — Schedule a Visit or Video Call
Meet the puppies — in person or online. We recommend a live video call for first-time buyers.
What happens:
Family chooses a puppy (or puppies) and clicks “Schedule Visit / Video Call”.
A Puppy Connection Team Member will coordinate a time that works for everyone.
For video calls we recommend: 10–15 minutes for introductions, ask/answer questions, and watch the puppies play and interact.
For in-person visits, we can arrange a meeting either at the breeder's home, or at a convenient location for both parties. Breeder, buyer, and puppy safety and comfortability is of utmost concern.
Q: Is the deposit refundable?
A: Deposits are refundable under specific conditions (breeder cancellation or serious vet findings). See the deposit policy for full details.
Q: Why do you require a deposit?
A: Deposits reduce no-shows, protect breeders from last-minute cancellations, and indicate buyer commitment.
Q: Can I change the puppy I reserved?
A: Yes — contact us. Transfers between litters or siblings are permitted depending on timing and breeder approval.
Step 2 — Deposit/Reservation
Reserve your chosen puppy with a secure deposit — we hold the puppy for you while final checks are completed.
Rest easy knowing that your chosen puppy is reserved just for you!
A deposit of $500 secures the puppy and allows us to proceed with travel or pick-up arrangements.
Deposits are processed securely on our website through Paypal, credit card, or Venmo.
Once deposit is received, the buyer gets a receipt and an estimated timeline for the go-home date.
Deposit policy​
Refunds: Deposits are non-refundable, except in the event of a grave health concern.
Transferability: Deposits are transferable to another puppy from the same breeder if mutually agreed.
Step 3 — Arrange Delivery or Meet Up
Convenient, safe delivery or local pickup
Options available:
Local pickup: Buyer picks up the puppy from the breeder per scheduled appointment. We provide a checklist to ensure smooth handoff.
Transport via Furry Freight: They run scheduled routes and provide temperature-controlled crates, documentation, and photo checks at handoff.
Special Delivery: This option is available to anyone who wants to get their puppy in between the regular routes. This option provides hand-delivery of your puppy by a puppy chaperone, meaning they get specialized attention throughout the travel (additional fees apply).
What you’ll receive at handoff:
Copy of health certificate and vaccine records
Microchip ID and registration forms (if applicable to your puppy)
Starter food
Toy/Blanket and any additional supplies included
A written care sheet and breeder’s contact details for follow-ups
​ ​
Q: How long does delivery take?
A: It depends on the route. We provide estimated windows and real-time tracking when available. Typical routes run every 2 weeks to the East Coast and South.
Q: What if the puppy gets stressed on the trip?
A: The drivers are trained to monitor puppies, ensuring their comfort during the entire trip. We also provide a care protocol to the buyer upon arrival. Any signs of prolonged distress prompt a vet check.
Q: Do you ship puppies in cargo on planes?
A: We avoid cargo travel for young puppies. If air travel is required, we prefer to use vetted flight nannies and direct flights only — this is arranged case-by-case and involves additional screening and fees.
Post Adoption Support
We don’t disappear after delivery — we offer lifetime support to ensure a happy match.
Support for first-time puppy owners (crate training, house training tips)
We LOVE getting updates! Send all your cute, funny, and downright adorable photos and videos our way.
Return / rehoming assistance if life circumstances change (we offer to help rehome or take the puppy back in limited circumstances )
Q: What support is available for behavior problems?
A: We provide general guidance and can recommend trainers or behaviorists. For complex issues, we can help refer to experts.
Q: What if the puppy has a health issue after we take them home?
A: If a serious health issue arises within the guarantee period, submit a claim with vet records and photos. We’ll review and work with the breeder on remedies, refunds, or replacement per policy.
Q: How do I register my puppy’s microchip/transfer ownership?
A: The breeder provides step-by-step instructions and forms at pickup/delivery. If you need help, our team can offer assistance in completing the transfer.
Have questions? Give us a call or send a message! We are happy to help!

Why Puppy Connection?
At Puppy Connection, we cherish the unique bond between dogs and humans. We recognize that dogs are more than pets; they are lifelong companions deserving of our utmost love and care. That’s why we and our network of breeders here in northern Indiana are committed to responsibly breeding, raising, and placing puppies into loving, forever homes. In a world where scams are on the rise, we have dedicated ourselves to providing a secure and trustworthy platform for small family breeders to showcase their delightful puppies and for families to find their perfect new furry friend. We have created a safe, easy program for breeders and families alike, ensuring that you can bring home your new puppy with confidence!
