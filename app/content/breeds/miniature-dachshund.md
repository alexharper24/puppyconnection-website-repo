---
breed: miniature-dachshund
intro: AKC recognizes two Dachshund sizes, and the Miniature is the smaller one, a long-backed hound with a big voice.
---
Tiny. | A Miniature weighs 11 pounds or under and stands 5 to 6 inches at the shoulder.
Three coats. | It comes smooth, wirehaired or longhaired, and AKC says longhaired coats may need more brushing.
Vocal. | AKC scores Dachshunds 5 of 5 for barking and 3 of 5 with young children.
Protect the back. | Keeping a Dachshund lean and watching its activity protects the long back, according to AKC.
