Lonnie
Lonnie Miller of Maple Lane Pets raises Papillons, Shih Poos, Shih Tzus, and Cavapoos on a peaceful ten-acre property, where maple trees line the drive and set the tone for daily life. Lonnie and his wife, Michelle, raise their family there with their young son, Seth, and their dogs are very much part of that rhythm.
Daily exercise and interaction are a priority. The dogs enjoy a large play yard, and little Seth is often right alongside them—playing, watching, and helping in his own way. This regular, gentle exposure to both adults and a child helps puppies grow comfortable with people and everyday activity.
Lonnie’s philosophy centers on starting with quality. He has invested in strong breeding dogs with the goal of producing healthy, happy parents—and in turn, healthy, happy puppies. Customer satisfaction matters to him, and he believes that well-cared-for adults are the foundation of good family companions.
Puppies are born in a clean, warm kennel with heated flooring and are handled and played with often by both adults and Seth. Vaccinations are administered at six, eight, and eleven weeks of age, and Lonnie works closely with his veterinarian to follow an effective deworming schedule beginning at two weeks of age.
Each puppy goes home with a complete vaccination and deworming record, a health certificate when required, starter food with feeding instructions, and a familiar toy and blanket from home.
Families looking for a puppy raised in a calm, family-centered setting—where playtime, fresh air, and daily care are part of the routine—are welcome to learn more about life at Maple Lane Pets.
We reserve Sundays for church and family, so we are not available for phone calls or visits on that day.
