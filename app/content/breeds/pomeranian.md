---
breed: pomeranian
intro: The Pomeranian is a tiny dog from the AKC Toy Group, with a plumed tail and a thick double coat that forms a ruff at the neck.
---
Tiny. | Grown Pomeranians weigh 3 to 7 pounds and stand 6 to 7 inches at the shoulder.
Regular brushing. | AKC scores shedding 2 of 5 and grooming 3, so the thick coat needs regular brushing.
Older kids. | AKC scores the breed 3 of 5 with young children, and a dog this small is easily hurt.
Long life. | Poms live 12 to 16 years, and parents should have knee, heart and eye checks.
