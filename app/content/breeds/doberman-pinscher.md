---
breed: doberman-pinscher
intro: The Doberman Pinscher is a large, short-coated Working Group dog that comes in black, red, blue or fawn with rust markings.
---
Large. | Grown Dobermans weigh 60 to 100 pounds and stand 24 to 28 inches at the shoulder.
High energy. | AKC scores the breed 5 of 5 for energy and trainability, so it needs daily exercise and training.
Family dog. | AKC rates the Doberman 5 of 5 with young children.
Genetic testing. | AKC's advice is to buy only from a breeder who has run genetic tests on both parents.
