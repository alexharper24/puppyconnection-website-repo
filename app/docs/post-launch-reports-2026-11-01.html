Town and breed searches after launch

Two reports for Alex before anything is built. The figures are Wix's own Search Console reports for the old site, read on October 12, 2026, and marked as measured or estimated where they appear.

1. Searches for puppies in Mishawaka, Lafayette and Elkhart

Over the year to October 11, 2026 the Wix site ranked near the top of Google for three town searches and drew 112 clicks from them. No page on the new site names any of the three towns, so these are the clicks most at risk.

SearchImpressionsClicksAverage position
puppies for sale mishawaka261433.1
puppies for sale lafayette indiana473353.4
puppies for sale elkhart169345.2

Measured, Wix Search Console, October 12, 2025 to October 11, 2026. August to December 2025 alone held 25 town and state searches with 203 clicks between them, among them Valparaiso, Richmond and Plymouth.

What drove those rankings

  • The page that took the clicks is not in the cached data, because Wix reports searches and pages separately. The home page took 92% of all page clicks from August to December 2025, so it most likely took these too (estimate). Only the Search Console account Wix connected can confirm it, with a search filter on each town.
  • The old copy did not name the towns. The Wix pages we saved say "northern Indiana" and name no town, and three breeder pages name LaGrange, Shipshewana or Goshen (measured on the saved copy).
  • The old listings did not carry a breeder town. None of the 273 saved Wix listings has a town field or a town in its description (measured).
  • The Business Profile sets its service area by state. Business Profile Manager lists Ohio, Florida and eight more areas for Puppy Connection, read on November 1, 2026. The other eight did not load, so whether any is a town is still open.

So the rankings most likely came from Google placing a northern Indiana home page near searchers in those towns, rather than from anything the pages said (estimate).

Where the breeders really are

The 41 kennels on the live site list Topeka (5), Goshen (4), LaGrange (3), Milford (3), Millersburg (2), Syracuse (2), Ligonier (2), and Wakarusa, Nappanee, Warsaw, Shipshewana and Rochester (1 each) in Indiana, plus kennels in Pennsylvania, Illinois, Michigan and Ohio. None lists Mishawaka, Elkhart or Lafayette. Goshen, Wakarusa, Millersburg and Nappanee are in or beside Elkhart County, and Mishawaka sits just west of it, while the nearest kennel to Lafayette is about an hour away (general geography, for Alex or Amber to confirm).

Proposal

  1. Show each breeder's town on the breeder page and on each puppy page, from the city and state the breeder already gives in the portal.
  2. Add a short section to the Indiana page that names the towns where breeders really are and says that families from Elkhart and Mishawaka are a short drive from the Goshen, Wakarusa and Millersburg kennels.
  3. Leave Lafayette out of the copy until a breeder near it lists, because no kennel is there and saying otherwise would mislead families.
  4. Ask Amber for the full service-area list on the Business Profile and whether to add Elkhart and Mishawaka to it.

Measured, Wix Search Console, August 1 to December 31, 2025. The batch file gives these as a year's total, but the cached report covers those five months only, so a full year would run higher (estimate).

Proposal

  1. Write a low-shedding guide that names the breeds on the site which shed little, with each claim checked at its source, and point the old hypoallergenic address at it. That page earned 11 of the 15 clicks, and "hypoallergenic puppies for sale" drew 550 impressions in the same window.
  2. Point each old breed address at that breed's own page in the redirect list now. The site already sends an address to the breeds page while its breed page is not built, as it does today for Basset Hound, so each one moves to its own page by itself on the first publish after a puppy of that breed lists.
  3. Leave Pug and Portuguese Water Dog as they are, since Google reported nothing for them.
