---
breed: shih-poo
intro: A Shih-Poo crosses a Shih Tzu with a Toy or Miniature Poodle. It has no AKC breed standard of its own, so a puppy's size and coat follow the two parents it comes from.
---
Small. | The parent breeds run 4 to 16 pounds, so ask whether the Poodle parent is a Toy or a Miniature.
Little shedding. | AKC scores the Shih Tzu and the Poodle 1 of 5 for shedding, and both coats need regular brushing.
Good with kids. | AKC gives the Shih Tzu and both Poodle sizes its top score with young children.
Check both parents. | Ask for the Poodle parent's patella, eye and PRA-prcd results, since AKC lists no club tests for the Shih Tzu.
