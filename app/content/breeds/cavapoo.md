---
breed: cavapoo
intro: A Cavapoo crosses a Cavalier King Charles Spaniel with a Miniature or Toy Poodle. No registry sets a standard for it, so each puppy's size and coat depend on its parents and generation.
---
Size varies. | The parent breeds weigh 4 to 18 pounds, and the Poodle size and generation shape where a puppy lands.
Coat varies. | A puppy can take after the low-shedding Poodle or the Cavalier, so shedding differs from puppy to puppy.
Family friendly. | AKC gives both the Cavalier and the Poodle its top score for young children.
Test both sides. | Ask for the Cavalier parent's heart results and a PRA-prcd DNA result for the Poodle.
