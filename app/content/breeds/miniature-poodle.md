---
breed: miniature-poodle
intro: The Miniature Poodle is the middle Poodle size, a curly-coated dog standing more than 10 and up to 15 inches tall.
---
Small. | Grown Miniatures weigh 10 to 15 pounds, and one over 15 inches tall counts as a Standard.
Barely sheds. | The curly coat scores 1 of 5 for shedding but 4 for grooming, so plan on regular clipping.
Easy to train. | AKC's trainability score for Poodles is 5 of 5, and they score the same with young children.
Long life. | Poodles live 10 to 18 years, and parents should have the PRA-prcd DNA test.
