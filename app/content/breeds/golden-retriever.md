---
breed: golden-retriever
intro: A Sporting Group breed, the Golden Retriever carries a double coat in shades of gold.
---
Large. | Grown Goldens weigh 55 to 75 pounds and stand 21.5 to 24 inches at the shoulder.
Sheds a lot. | AKC scores the double coat 4 of 5 for shedding, so brush it often.
Great with families. | Goldens get AKC's top score for trainability and young children, and its lowest for barking.
Health testing. | The breed club recommends hip, elbow, heart and eye checks plus an NCL DNA test for parents.
