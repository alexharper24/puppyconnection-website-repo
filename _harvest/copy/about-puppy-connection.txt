ABOUT PUPPY CONNECTION
Puppy Connection is a small group of reputable, small, family dog breeders based in northern Indiana. We have over 20 years experience in breeding, showing, and grooming and we, along with our associate breeders, are continually finding ways to enhance every aspect of breeding and raising puppies to make them the best family companions they can be. Our companions should be healthy, well-mannered, and a joy to live with!
Discover the Puppy Connection Difference
Choosing a new puppy is more than a purchase—it’s the beginning of a lifelong friendship. At Puppy Connection in northern Indiana, we make that first step joyful and worry-free by wrapping every puppy in top-tier care and ongoing support.
What Comes with Your Puppy
Comprehensive Health Start – Each puppy arrives with first vaccinations, dewormings, and a clean bill of health from a licensed veterinarian.
Smooth Transition Kit – We send you home with a starter bag of the premium food your puppy already enjoys, easing tummy troubles and keeping routines on track.
Social Foundations – Raised around children and other dogs, our pups learn early to be confident playmates and gentle family companions.
Lifetime Support – From late-night questions to training tips years down the road, our Puppy Connection team is just a call, text, or email away.
Health & Registry Peace of Mind
We go beyond the basics with optional registry packages and preventive measures such as genetic health testing. Plus, all parents are active, sociable dogs, laying the groundwork for puppies with sound bodies and sweet temperaments.
Convenient Pick-Up or Delivery
Whether you’re in the Midwest or along the East Coast, bringing your new companion home is effortless. Swing by our Indiana location for local pick-up, or let us arrange safe, doorstep delivery.
Guidance at Every Turn
Our knowledgeable staff is dedicated to making the adoption process simple and stress-free. Explore our blog for training guides, nutrition advice, and heartwarming stories, or reach out to our hotline anytime you need a helping hand.
Ready to meet your perfect match? Browse our current litters, and let Puppy Connection introduce you to a healthy, happy puppy eager to create a lifetime of memories with your family.
EXPERIENCE YOU CAN TRUST
We have over 20 years of experience in showing, breeding, grooming, photographing, and studying dogs, as well as teaching courses in grooming, breeding, conformation, and genetics. Our associate breeders attend ongoing breeder education seminars, along with gaining knowledge from fellow breeders and their veterinarians.
BREEDERS YOU CAN DEPEND ON
The breeders we choose to work with and feature all have one thing in common - they love their dogs! They seek to improve the breed's health and personality so that the next generation of family companions will live healthy lives and be a blessing to their new homes. For those that have children, they love to involve their kids in the day-to-day duties of caring for the dogs and puppies and working on their socialization with various toys, sounds, textures, and smells.
PUPPIES YOU CAN LOVE FOR A LIFETIME
Our breeders work very hard at choosing the right pairing when planning a litter, taking health, temperament, and conformation into consideration. When the puppies are born, they receive much care and attention to their health, growth, and socialization so that they will be ready for the transition to their new homes. All of our breeders work closely with their vet to ensure the proper vaccinations, dewormings, and puppy exams are completed before going home.
