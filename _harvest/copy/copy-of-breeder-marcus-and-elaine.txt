Perry
Perry Kuhns has been dedicated to the lovely Havanese breed for over ten years at Chain-O-Lakes Kennel. He and his wife, Sharon, along with their five children, settled on their twelve-acre horse farm in LaGrange, Indiana, in 2019—a setting that allows their dogs plenty of room to grow, play, and be part of daily family life.
Caring for the dogs is truly a family effort. Perry begins each morning tending to the dogs and managing health records, vaccinations, and deworming schedules. In the evenings, the children help with chores and spend time playing with the dogs and puppies, providing valuable socialization that helps produce confident, people-loving companions.
One area where Perry especially stands out is his attention to coat type and coat care. He is intentional about breeding Havanese with correct, manageable coats and spends time maintaining them so puppies are accustomed to regular handling and grooming. This early exposure helps puppies transition more smoothly into home grooming routines and sets families up for long-term success with the breed’s signature coat.
Perry’s breeding philosophy centers on raising healthy, happy family pets. Adult dogs are OFA certified for knees, eyes, and heart, and he avoids breeding traits that could negatively impact a puppy’s quality of life. His Havanese are most commonly sable/white and black/white. He also appreciates the breed’s non-shedding, hypoallergenic coat, which makes Havanese a wonderful option for many families with allergies.
Puppies receive structured, proactive veterinary care. They are given a Neopar vaccine at four weeks, followed by Bordetella and a 5-way vaccine at five weeks. Deworming includes Pyrantel at two weeks and Fenbendazole at four, six, and eight weeks. All puppies are microchipped and are typically ready to go home at eight weeks of age. Veterinary care is provided by Alpha Vet Clinic in Shipshewana and LaGrange Vet Clinic.
Puppies are born in a climate-controlled kennel and, around six to seven weeks of age, are introduced to indoor-outdoor access through a doggie door. They are exposed to a variety of environments—including the kennel, shop, and home—helping them become comfortable with everyday sights, sounds, and activity.
Each puppy goes home with a complete vaccination and deworming record, microchip documentation, a health certificate when required, starter food, and a familiar toy and blanket from home.
Perry enjoys connecting with families who value healthy, well-raised Havanese and is always happy to answer questions about his dogs, coats, and care routines. He believes communication and transparency are important, and is glad to serve as a reference and resource for families considering one of his puppies.
We reserve Sundays for church and family, so we are not available for phone calls or visits on that day.
