Puppy Connection breed list

The Wix export has 335 rows. After the merges below, the picker would hold 328 breeds, which is the 26 already on the site, 5 the site has that Wix does not, and 297 new ones.

Breeders can already list a breed with no page

The site makes a breed page by itself the first time one of that breed's puppies is listed, and it uses the puppy's photo until the breed has its own. So adding these breeds to the picker is all it takes, and a rare breed never shows an empty page.

Mixes, your decision

Recommended: keep one Mixed Breed choice (plus Doodle Mix) and leave the other 36 "Breed Mix" names off. A breeder lists a mixed puppy as Mixed Breed and names the mom and dad, which the portal already records, so the listing still says what is in the mix. That keeps a puppy from landing on a breed page of its own with one listing and nothing else, and keeps the picker short.

The other way: keep every "Breed Mix" name as its own breed, so a family searching "golden retriever mix puppies" can land on a page of them. Each page appears only once such a puppy is listed.
