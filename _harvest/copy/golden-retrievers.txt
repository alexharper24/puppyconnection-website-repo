About the Golden Retriever
The Golden Retriever is one of America’s most beloved family breeds — and for good reason! They are gentle, incredibly loyal, and naturally attuned to their people. Goldens thrive in fairly active homes where they can play, explore, and participate in daily family life.
Whether you’re looking for a kid-friendly companion, a therapy prospect, or a hiking buddy, Goldens bring an unmatched blend of sweetness and versatility.

How often do Goldens shed?
Golden Retrievers shed year-round, with heavier shedding during spring and fall. Their double coat naturally “blows out” twice a year, which is completely normal.
Regular brushing 2–3 times a week (daily during heavy shed seasons) helps keep loose hair under control and keeps their coat healthy.
If you love the Golden personality, a little fluff is well worth the joy they bring!
Are Goldens good with kids?
Absolutely — Goldens are one of the best family breeds in the world.
They’re naturally gentle, patient, and eager to please. Their steady temperament makes them excellent with kids of all ages, from toddlers to teens.
We begin early socialization so your puppy comes home confident, friendly, and well-prepared to join a busy household.
Do they require a fenced yard?
A fenced yard is helpful but not required.
Goldens do best with regular daily exercise, whether that’s a fenced yard, walks, playtime, or outdoor activities with their people.
What they truly need is:
space to stretch their legs
mental stimulation
consistent time with their family
They are people-oriented dogs and thrive when included in daily life.
How big do Goldens get?
Adult Golden Retrievers typically reach:
Males: 65–75 lbs
Females: 55–65 lbs
They grow into a sturdy, athletic medium-large size, with full maturity (muscle + coat) around 18–24 months.
