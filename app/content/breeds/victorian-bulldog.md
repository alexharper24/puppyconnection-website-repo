---
breed: victorian-bulldog
intro: AKC does not recognize the Victorian Bulldog as a breed, so no registry standard covers its size, coat or health testing.
---
Size varies. | With no standard, size follows the parents, so ask for their weights and to see them.
Ask about shedding. | No registry describes the coat, so ask how much the parents shed.
Ask about heat. | Ask how the parents handle heat, since heat is the first concern on AKC's Bulldog page.
Health records. | Ask for the parents' screening results, such as the patella and heart checks the Bulldog club recommends.
