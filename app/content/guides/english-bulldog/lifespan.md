AKC gives the English Bulldog, which it registers as the Bulldog, a life expectancy of 8 to 10 years. Veterinary records from the UK point a little lower, with a median of about 7.2 years in one large study of Bulldogs seen by their regular vets.

Those two figures come from different places, and the gap between them is worth understanding before you choose a puppy.

## What does the research say about Bulldog lifespan?

A study by VetCompass researchers at the Royal Veterinary College, published in 2019, read the clinical records of Bulldogs under primary veterinary care in the UK during 2013. It found a median longevity of 7.2 years, and males lived a shorter median than females, at 6.7 years against 7.9.

A later study from the same group, published in 2022, built life tables for dogs that died between 2016 and 2020 and put the English Bulldog's life expectancy at birth at 7.39 years.

AKC's breed page gives its range without a method, while the UK figures are measured from vet records, so the two are not directly comparable. Still, half the Bulldogs that died in the 2019 study were younger than 7.2 years, which sits below the start of AKC's range, and that is a reason to look closely at the parents' health.

## Which health problems affect English Bulldogs?

In the 2019 study, the most common disorders recorded in Bulldogs were ear canal inflammation, called otitis externa, skin infection, called pyoderma, and being overweight. The authors linked many of the common disorders to the breed's appearance, including skin fold disease, the breathing condition called brachycephalic obstructive airway syndrome, and ulcers on the surface of the eye.

AKC's own care advice centers on heat. It says owners should make sure a Bulldog does not overheat, should not leave it in hot sun without shade and water, and should never leave it in an enclosed car in even mildly warm weather. A Bulldog that is overexcited or breathing too hard will have its tongue hanging out unusually far, with a bluish cast instead of normal pink, and AKC says soaking the dog with cool water and offering ice can help cool it.

## What health testing should a Bulldog's parents have?

The national breed club recommends three exams for breeding Bulldogs, which are a patella evaluation, a cardiac exam, and a radiographic evaluation for tracheal hypoplasia. Ask the breeder which of these both parents have had and whether you can see the results.

Ask how the parents breathe at rest and after play, and how each one copes with warm weather.

## What can an owner do?

The 2019 study described the problems Bulldogs had rather than testing ways to prevent them, but its findings and AKC's advice point to the same care. Keep the dog at a lean weight and out of the heat, and have your vet check its skin folds, ears and eyes at each visit. The World Small Animal Veterinary Association's body condition chart describes an ideal dog as one whose ribs you can feel easily under minimal fat, with a waist visible from above. Your vet can score your Bulldog against it.

## What does the breed standard describe?

The standard describes the size for a mature male as about 50 pounds and a mature female as about 40 pounds. It also disqualifies the merle pattern and any color or marking it does not define, so a merle Bulldog is outside the breed standard.
