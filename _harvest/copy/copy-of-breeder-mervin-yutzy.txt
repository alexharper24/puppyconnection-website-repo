Marcus and Elaine
Marcus and Elaine Miller have been raising Miniature Schnauzers for the past eight years, with a strong focus on health, structure, and family-friendly temperaments. In early 2026, they moved to their new five-acre home, where their dogs are cared for in a dedicated 12 x 16 ft facility featuring outdoor runs and comfortable shade.
For Marcus and Elaine, daily care
is a top priority. The dogs receive
fresh food and water every day,
runs are cleaned routinely, and
the entire area is sanitized to
maintain a healthy environment.
As their setup continues to grow,
the Millers plan to allow their dogs
supervised time roaming the property.
Their breeding philosophy centers on raising strong, well-balanced Miniature Schnauzers that stay true to breed standards. Marcus and Elaine intentionally focus on producing healthy dogs with bold markings, a stocky build, and the kind of temperament that makes a wonderful family companion. Their Schnauzers stay between 12-18 lbs.
Puppies are born indoors in a heated
building and are gradually introduced to                                                 the outdoors as they mature. The                                                                  Millers spend time with their dogs                                                                daily, especially during feeding, and                                                              puppies are gently socialized, including                                                        exposure to small children. Each puppy is microchipped and receives a 5-way vaccine at six weeks of age.
Puppies go home with a complete and accurate vaccination and deworming record, microchip registration, a health certificate when required, starter food, and a familiar toy and blanket from home. All veterinary care is provided through Alpha Vet Clinic in Shipshewana.
The Millers are always happy to
connect with families who value healthy,
well-raised Miniature Schnauzers and
to answer any questions about their dogs
or program. They believe open
communication is important are glad to
be a reference and resource for families
considering one of their puppies.
We reserve Sundays for church and family, so we are not available for phone calls or visits on that day.
