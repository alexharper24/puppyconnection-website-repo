---
breed: cavalier-king-charles-spaniel
intro: The Cavalier King Charles Spaniel is a small spaniel from the AKC Toy Group, known for its silky coat and friendly nature.
---
Small. | Grown Cavaliers stand 12 to 13 inches and weigh 13 to 18 pounds.
Some shedding. | AKC scores the silky coat 2 of 5 for shedding, and it still needs regular brushing.
Great with kids. | AKC puts the Cavalier at 5 of 5 around young children.
Heart testing. | The breed is prone to mitral valve heart disease, so ask for the parents' cardiac results.
