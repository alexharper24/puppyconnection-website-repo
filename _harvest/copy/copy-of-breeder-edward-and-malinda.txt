Tyler and Eileen
Tyler and Eileen Martin have been raising 25–30 lb Pembroke Welsh Corgis for the past four years on their 17-acre property. They live in a ranch-style home and have thoughtfully set up a warm, comfortable space where their dogs are raised and cared for. With plenty of open pasture, their corgis have room to do what they do best—run, play, chase balls, and greet their people with enthusiasm every time they appear.
The Martins have created an environment that fits the boundless energy and curiosity of the corgi breed. Daily life includes romping in the pasture, interactive play, and plenty of human connection. The result is a group of very likeable, people-oriented dogs who enjoy both activity and attention.
Eileen has a special love for puppies and finds joy in every stage of their development. From the quiet early days to the moment they discover toys and begin bouncing from one activity to the next, she takes hands-on pleasure in watching their personalities unfold. She spends time with the puppies each morning and evening, making sure they receive consistent attention, interaction, and care.
Puppies are born in the kennel, where they are given a calm, private start to life. Socialization begins early and continues daily. When weather allows, Eileen brings the puppies outside to play in the yard; during colder months, playtime and interaction continue indoors in the kennel. Vaccinations begin after six weeks of age, and deworming is provided every two weeks.
Health testing is an important part of the Martins’ program. They regularly invest in OFA certifications for hearts, hips, elbows, and eyes to support the long-term health and soundness of their dogs.
Families who love the lively, affectionate nature of corgis and appreciate puppies raised with energy, attention, and genuine enthusiasm are invited to learn more about the Martins’ program.
We reserve Sundays for church and family, so we are not available for phone calls or visits on that day.
