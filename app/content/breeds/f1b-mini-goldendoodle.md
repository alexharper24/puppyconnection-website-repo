---
breed: f1b-mini-goldendoodle
intro: An F1b Mini Goldendoodle comes from a Goldendoodle bred back to a Golden Retriever or a Poodle, with a Miniature Poodle in the line to keep it small. Because no registry sets a doodle standard, its size and coat follow the parents and the generation.
---
Size varies. | Parents set the size, and the Goldendoodle Association's Mini runs 14 to 17 inches, typically 26 to 35 pounds.
Coat varies. | Shedding differs from puppy to puppy, because the Golden scores 4 of 5 for shedding and the Poodle 1.
Family friendly. | Goldens and Poodles both earn AKC's top marks for trainability and for young children.
Test both sides. | Ask for the Golden's hip, elbow and heart results and the Poodle's PRA-prcd DNA test.
