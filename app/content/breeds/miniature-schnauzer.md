---
breed: miniature-schnauzer
intro: The Miniature Schnauzer is a sturdy little terrier with a wiry double coat and the bushy eyebrows and beard that mark the breed.
---
Small. | Grown Schnauzers stand 12 to 14 inches and weigh 11 to 20 pounds.
Needs trimming. | Shedding is moderate at 3 of 5, but the wiry coat needs regular trimming to keep its shape.
Friendly and vocal. | Schnauzers get top marks from AKC both with young children and for barking.
Long life. | Schnauzers live 12 to 15 years, and AKC lists pancreatitis and urinary stones among their health concerns.
