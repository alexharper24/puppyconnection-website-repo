---
breed: maltese
intro: The Maltese is a toy dog with a long, silky single coat that the breed standard asks to be pure white.
---
Tiny. | Grown Maltese stand 7 to 9 inches and weigh under 7 pounds, with 4 to 6 preferred.
Barely sheds. | AKC scores shedding 1 of 5, but the long coat needs gentle brushing every day.
Affectionate. | AKC scores the Maltese 5 of 5 with family and 3 of 5 with young children.
Long life. | Maltese live 12 to 15 years, and the club advises knee, heart and bile acid tests.
