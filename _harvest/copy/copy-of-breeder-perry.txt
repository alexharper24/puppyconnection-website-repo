Cozy Pine Canine
Gerald Bontrager of Cozy Pine Canine has been raising Shih Poos, Cavapoos, Poodles, and Biewer Terriers for the past nine years. He and his wife, Kayla, raise their family on a six-acre farmette where ducks, horses, puppies, and a garden full of organic food are all part of everyday life. Their two children grow up right alongside the animals, learning responsibility, kindness, and a genuine love for small critters of all kinds.
Animals are woven into the rhythm of the Bontrager household. From daily chores to quiet moments of interaction, puppies are raised in an environment where being handled, observed, and cared for is simply part of normal family life. This consistent, gentle exposure helps produce puppies that are comfortable, curious, and well-adjusted.
Gerald’s philosophy centers on raising healthy puppies for real families. He places strong emphasis on sound structure, beautiful coats, and attractive color markings, while never losing sight of what matters most—long-term health and quality of life. A key way he sets his program apart is through his commitment to genetic health. Gerald invests in comprehensive DNA panel testing, including color DNA screening, to help ensure puppies are clear of known genetic issues and to make informed breeding decisions.
Puppies are born and raised in a clean, carefully maintained environment that is washed daily and disinfected weekly. Socialization begins early, with puppies spending time outdoors in both sun and shade when weather allows. During inclement weather, puppies are kept indoors and played with on the floor, ensuring they continue to receive daily interaction and enrichment.
Vaccinations are administered at six, eight, and ten weeks of age, and dewormer is rotated every two weeks. All puppies are microchipped. Most puppies are ready to go home around ten weeks of age, though some may be ready as early as eight weeks depending on size and development. Gerald’s dogs also have their eyes certified free of disease by an OFA-affiliated ophthalmologist. Veterinary care is provided through Alpha Vet Clinic in Shipshewana.
Each puppy goes home with a complete vaccination and deworming record, a health certificate when required, microchip documentation, starter food with feeding instructions, and a familiar toy and blanket from home.
Gerald and Kayla enjoy connecting with families who value thoughtfully raised puppies and responsible breeding practices. Those interested in a puppy raised with intention, care, and a whole lot of animal love are welcome to get in touch.
We reserve Sundays for church and family, so we are not available for phone calls or visits on that day.
