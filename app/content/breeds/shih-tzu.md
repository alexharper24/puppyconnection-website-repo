---
breed: shih-tzu
intro: The Shih Tzu is a toy-sized companion with a long double coat, and the breed standard accepts every color.
---
Small. | Grown Shih Tzus stand 9 to 10.5 inches and weigh 9 to 16 pounds.
Barely sheds. | AKC scores shedding 1 of 5, but grooming scores 4, so the long coat needs frequent brushing.
Good with families. | AKC rates the Shih Tzu 5 of 5 with young children.
Long life. | Shih Tzus can live 10 to 18 years, and AKC notes they handle heat poorly.
