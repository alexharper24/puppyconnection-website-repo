Questions breeders ask

Selling puppies online with Puppy Connection

Where can I sell my puppies online?

Puppy Connection is a directory for small family breeders, starting in Indiana and the Midwest. Each puppy gets its own page with your photos, price and dates, it shows on its breed page and your breeder page, and families contact you directly. You sell the puppy yourself, so the sale, the deposit and the paperwork stay with you.

How much does it cost to advertise puppies here?

$14.99 per puppy, paid once. There is no commission and no cut of your sale, and you can pay for a whole litter in one checkout. A listing has no end date and nothing to renew. It stays up while you add a new photo of the puppy at least every four weeks, until you mark the puppy placed or take it down.

How do buyers reach me?

Every puppy page shows your phone, your email, your website and your Facebook page if you add them, and can link straight to that puppy's page on your own site. Your breeder portal shows how many families viewed each puppy and clicked through to contact you.

What makes a puppy listing work?

Clear photos of the actual puppy, an honest price, the birth and go-home dates, and what comes home with the puppy, such as a vet exam, a microchip or registration. Families compare listings side by side, and the complete ones get the calls.
