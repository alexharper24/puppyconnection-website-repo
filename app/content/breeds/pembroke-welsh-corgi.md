---
breed: pembroke-welsh-corgi
intro: The Pembroke Welsh Corgi is a short-legged herding dog from the AKC Herding Group with a thick double coat.
---
Compact. | Corgis stand 10 to 12 inches, and AKC caps males at 30 pounds and females at 28.
Sheds a lot. | AKC scores the short double coat 4 of 5 for shedding, so expect regular brushing.
Busy herder. | AKC gives the Corgi 4 of 5 for energy, for trainability and for barking.
Health checks. | Expect 12 to 13 years, and ask for the parents' hip and eye results, as the breed club advises.
