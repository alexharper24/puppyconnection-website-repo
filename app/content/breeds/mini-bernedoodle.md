---
breed: mini-bernedoodle
intro: A Mini Bernedoodle is a Bernese Mountain Dog and Miniature Poodle cross. Mini is a breeder's label rather than a registry size, so expect size and coat to vary with the parents and generation.
---
Size varies. | No standard defines a Mini, so the parents' weights are the best guide to adult size.
Coat varies. | A Bernese scores 5 of 5 for shedding and a Poodle 1, so a litter can range widely.
Good with families. | Both parent breeds score 5 of 5 with young children and 4 of 5 for energy.
Test both sides. | Ask for hip and elbow results on the Bernese parent and a PRA-prcd test on the Poodle.
