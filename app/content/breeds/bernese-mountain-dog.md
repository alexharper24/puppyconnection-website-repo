---
breed: bernese-mountain-dog
intro: Known as the Berner, the Bernese Mountain Dog is a big Working Group dog with a thick black, rust and white coat.
---
Big. | Grown Berners weigh 70 to 115 pounds, so plan for a large dog.
Sheds heavily. | The thick double coat needs regular brushing all year.
Great with families. | AKC rates the breed 5 of 5 with young children.
Shorter life. | Usually 7 to 10 years, so health testing on the parents matters.
