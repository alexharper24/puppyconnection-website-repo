Marvin
We having been raising puppies for over 5 years now. We enjoy living on a hobby farm. I grew with animals and really enjoy the time I get to spend with my dogs. My children also love playing with our puppies and the puppies will be used to being around children.
We love our dogs and we aim to raise happy and healthy companions for your family.
Our puppies are taken to the vet for a full physical exam when they are 6 to 8  weeks of age.  We remove declaws and dock tails depending if its breed appropriate.
We do our very best to keep our dogs and puppies healthy and happy.
Vaccination Protocol - They will have their puppy deworming and vaccinations started prior to going home.
What we send home  -
We send our puppies home with a vaccination record and a health guarantee.
We reserve Sundays for church and family, so we are not available for phone calls or visits on that day.
