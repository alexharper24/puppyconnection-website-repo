Portie Breed Information
The Portuguese Water Dog: A Spirited Swimmer with a Heart of Gold
Energetic, intelligent, and loyal to the core, the Portuguese Water Dog (often called a “Portie”) is a fantastic companion for active families and water lovers alike. Originally bred to work alongside fishermen on the coast of Portugal, these dogs are strong, agile, and happiest when they have a job to do—whether it’s retrieving from the water, learning new tricks, or simply being your fun-loving shadow.
A Distinctive and Athletic Build
Portuguese Water Dogs are medium-sized, well-muscled dogs with a robust frame built for action. Standing 17 to 23 inches tall and weighing between 35 to 60 pounds, they’re compact yet powerful swimmers, thanks to their webbed feet and athletic build.
Their curly or wavy coats come in black, white, brown, or black-and-white combinations and are hypoallergenic for many allergy sufferers. While they don’t shed much, regular grooming is essential to prevent matting and keep them looking sharp—especially if you go for the traditional “lion cut” or “retriever clip.”
A Lively and Loyal Personality
Porties are full of energy and enthusiasm. They’re incredibly smart, eager to please, and highly trainable—traits that made them indispensable on fishing boats and still make them excellent at obedience, agility, and water sports today.
They form strong bonds with their people and love to be part of the family’s daily activities. Whether you’re going on an adventure or just hanging out at home, a Portuguese Water Dog is happiest when they’re right by your side. Expect a dog that’s both affectionate and a bit goofy, always ready to make you smile with their expressive eyes and cheerful nature.
Ideal for Active Families and Outdoor Lovers
Portuguese Water Dogs are a great fit for active households who can match their high energy and need for mental stimulation. They do best with plenty of exercise, whether that’s through swimming, hiking, long walks, or structured play. Without it, they might find their own (less desirable) ways to stay busy—like chewing, digging, or redecorating your yard.
They’re wonderful with children and usually get along well with other pets, especially with proper socialization. Their fun-loving temperament and sturdy build make them great companions for kids who want an active playmate and loyal friend.
Smart, Trainable, and Ready to Work
Thanks to their working-dog background, Porties thrive on tasks and training. They pick up commands quickly and genuinely enjoy learning. From basic obedience to advanced tricks, they love the mental challenge and the opportunity to impress their humans.
Consistency, positive reinforcement, and variety go a long way in keeping their clever minds engaged. Whether you’re training for fun or competition, a Portuguese Water Dog is up for the challenge.

Top 5 Frequently Asked Questions About Portuguese Water Dogs

Are Porties good family dogs?
Yes! Portuguese Water Dogs are wonderful family companions—affectionate, loyal, and highly people-oriented. They tend to bond very closely with their families and are great with children thanks to their playful yet gentle nature. They typically get along well with other dogs too, especially when socialized early. Because they’re smart and active, they thrive in homes where they get lots of attention, mental engagement, and physical activity. If you're looking for a friendly, energetic dog that truly wants to be part of the family, a Portie might be the perfect fit.
Do Porties require a lot of grooming?
Yes, grooming is an important part of Portie care. Their curly or wavy coat doesn’t shed much (a big plus for allergy-sensitive households), but it does require regular maintenance to stay healthy and mat-free. Brushing several times a week is recommended, along with professional grooming every 6–8 weeks to keep their coat neat and manageable. Many owners opt for the traditional “lion clip” or a more practical retriever clip. Routine care like ear cleaning and dental hygiene is important too—especially since they love the water and can be prone to ear issues.
Are Porties easy to train?
Absolutely! These dogs are incredibly intelligent and eager to learn. They were bred to work closely with humans—retrieving nets, herding fish, and even delivering messages on boats—so they love having a job and excel in training environments. Porties respond very well to positive reinforcement, and they thrive when training is fun and challenging. They do best with confident, consistent leadership and early socialization. They’re excellent candidates for obedience, agility, rally, dock diving, and even therapy work.
Do Porties have health issues?
While generally healthy and long-lived (often 12–15 years), they can be prone to a few inherited conditions. The most common concerns include hip dysplasia, progressive retinal atrophy (PRA), and juvenile dilated cardiomyopathy. Responsible breeders test for these conditions and more, which is a big step toward ensuring a healthy puppy. Routine vet care, a high-quality diet, and plenty of exercise all contribute to keeping your Portie in great shape.
How much exercise do Porties need?
Quite a bit—they are a high-energy working breed that needs plenty of physical and mental stimulation each day. At least an hour of exercise daily is ideal, including walks, swimming (they love water!), fetch, and interactive play. They also need puzzle toys, training games, or structured activities to keep their sharp minds busy. A bored Portie can become destructive or overly vocal, so active families or individuals who enjoy time outdoors are a great match for this breed.
