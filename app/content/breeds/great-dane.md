---
breed: great-dane
intro: The Great Dane is a giant Working Group dog with a short, smooth coat in seven standard colors, including harlequin and mantle.
---
Giant. | Grown Danes weigh 110 to 175 pounds and stand 28 to 32 inches at the shoulder.
Easy coat. | The short coat gets AKC's lowest grooming score, and shedding sits in the middle at 3 of 5.
Train early. | AKC scores the Dane 3 of 5 with young children, and its size calls for early manners.
Shorter life. | Danes usually live 7 to 10 years, and AKC calls bloat the breed's number-one killer.
