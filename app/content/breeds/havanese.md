---
breed: havanese
intro: The Havanese is a little Toy Group dog with a long, silky, wavy coat that the breed standard accepts in any color.
---
Small. | Grown Havanese stand 8.5 to 11.5 inches and weigh 7 to 13 pounds.
Low shedding. | AKC scores shedding 2 of 5, but the long coat mats without regular brushing.
Kid friendly. | AKC's score with young children is 5 of 5, though barking scores 4.
Lives long. | Havanese usually reach 14 to 16 years, and the club advises knee, hip and eye exams for breeding dogs.
