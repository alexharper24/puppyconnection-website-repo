---
breed: teddy-bear-shihchon
intro: A Teddy Bear, also called a Shichon or Zuchon, crosses a Shih Tzu with a Bichon Frise. There is no AKC standard for it, so its size and coat come from the parents and the generation.
---
Small. | The parent breeds weigh 9 to 18 pounds, so ask what this puppy's parents weigh.
Low-shedding parents. | Both parents shed little, but the coat needs regular brushing and trims to avoid mats.
Good with kids. | The Shih Tzu side earns AKC's top score with young children.
Ask about health. | AKC lists no club health tests for the Shih Tzu, so ask what each parent was checked for.
