---
breed: mini-aussiedoodle
intro: A Mini Aussiedoodle is a Poodle crossed with either an Australian Shepherd or the smaller Miniature American Shepherd. With no AKC standard behind it, size and coat change with the parents and generation.
---
Size varies. | The parent breeds range from 10 to 65 pounds, so ask what each parent weighs.
Coat varies. | The shepherds score 3 of 5 for shedding and the Poodle 1, so puppies in one litter can differ.
High energy. | AKC scores both shepherd breeds 5 of 5 for energy, so plan on daily exercise and training.
MDR1 test. | Ask for the MDR1 result, a drug sensitivity DNA test on the Miniature American Shepherd club's list.
