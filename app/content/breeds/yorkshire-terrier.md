---
breed: yorkshire-terrier
intro: The Yorkshire Terrier, or Yorkie, is a tiny Toy Group dog with a long, silky coat that goes from black and tan in puppies to blue and tan in adults.
---
Tiny. | Grown Yorkies stand 7 to 8 inches and weigh no more than 7 pounds under the breed standard.
Daily grooming. | Shedding scores just 1 of 5, but grooming scores 5, so a long coat needs daily brushing.
Lively and vocal. | Yorkies score 4 of 5 for both energy and barking on AKC's scale, and 5 with young children.
Long life. | A Yorkie lives 11 to 15 years, and its parents should have patella and eye exams.
