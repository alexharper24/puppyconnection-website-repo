Puppies whose Wix photos changed

Every Wix puppy page was read on November 1, 2026 from lablakeslee.wixsite.com/puppyconnection (275 pages, 6 unreadable) and its photos compared, by Wix photo id, with the photos Puppy Connection imported for that puppy on October 2.

104 puppies still listed for sale on Puppy Connection have photos on Wix that we do not show. For 12 of them the breeder replaced the whole set on Wix, so none of our photos are on Wix any more. The rest have some photos swapped. A further 32 adopted or archived puppies changed too, and 28 listed puppies only have extra photos past the 10 we imported, with every photo we show still current.
