---
breed: french-bulldog
intro: The French Bulldog, or Frenchie, is a small, short-faced dog known for its large, upright bat ears.
---
Small. | Grown Frenchies stand 11 to 13 inches and weigh under 28 pounds.
Easy coat. | The short coat needs about a weekly brushing, and AKC scores its shedding 3 of 5.
Quiet and friendly. | AKC scores the Frenchie 1 of 5 for barking and 5 of 5 with young children.
Heat and water. | Like other flat-faced breeds it can struggle in heat, and AKC warns Frenchies cannot swim.
