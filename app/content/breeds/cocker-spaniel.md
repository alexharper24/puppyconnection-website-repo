---
breed: cocker-spaniel
intro: This page covers the American Cocker Spaniel, a Sporting Group breed with a long, silky coat, which AKC registers apart from the English Cocker.
---
Medium small. | Grown Cockers stand 13.5 to 15.5 inches and weigh 20 to 30 pounds.
Regular grooming. | The long, silky coat needs regular brushing and trimming, and the ears need cleaning.
Gentle. | AKC describes the breed as gentle and good with children and with other dogs.
Long lived. | AKC gives 10 to 14 years, and the breed club suggests hip and eye exams for parents.
