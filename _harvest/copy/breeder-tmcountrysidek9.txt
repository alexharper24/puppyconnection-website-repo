Giant breeds have been a passion of ours for the past 17 years. We love the Great Dane breed with their beautiful coat colors, great temperaments, intelligence, and companionship. Occasionally we have Daniff (Great Dane/Mastiff) puppies because we love the hybrid vigor and the gentleness they bestow. Our adult dogs are vet checked regularly, have a lot of hands-on care, and are well-taken care of.  They have access to both indoors and outdoors. We take extra care to ensure that our nursing mothers are comfortable while raising their babies. The puppies are given lots of hands-on care and as they grow our family makes sure they are acclimated to various environments and experiences. At 6 weeks we start on basic training which includes crate-training, going potty outside, and walking on a leash. Before our puppies leave for their new homes, they are vet checked, microchipped, and UTD on vaccinations and dewormings. They will go to their new homes with their registration papers, health guarantee, a puppy care package, and lifetime support from us. Our goal is to provide a happy, health puppy to a fur-ever family that will keep on loving our fur-babies.
We reserve Sundays for church and family, so we are not available for phone calls or visits on that day.
We are required to charge sales tax for any family that comes to personally pick up their puppy.
