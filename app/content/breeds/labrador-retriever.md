---
breed: labrador-retriever
intro: The Labrador Retriever is a strongly built retriever with a short, dense double coat, bred to work beside hunters and fishermen.
---
Large. | Grown Labs weigh 55 to 80 pounds and stand 21.5 to 24.5 inches at the shoulder.
Heavy shedder. | The thick double coat rates 4 of 5 for shedding, though AKC puts grooming at a simple 2.
Active family dog. | Labs score 5 of 5 on AKC's scales for energy, trainability and young children.
Health testing. | Labs live 11 to 13 years, and parents should have hip, elbow and eye exams plus the EIC DNA test.
